# Asset manifest

Archive photographs are preserved in `assets/archive/`. The `/archive-media/[filename]` route serves them to local review builds. For public builds it returns 404 until the permissions are cleared and `RK_APPROVED_ARCHIVE_MEDIA=1` is set. Archive images illustrate pattern or process and are captioned as references. They do not establish current inventory.
