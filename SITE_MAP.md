## Home page journey

1. **Hero** `#top` — A photographic first impression and the hand-printing proposition.
2. **The story** `#story` — Bagru, the Derawala family and the philosophy of handmade work.
3. **The making** `#process` — Four interactive stages: block, impression, colour and cloth.
4. **Heritage interlude** — An illustrated pause on variation and the maker's trace.
5. **Craft journal** `#journal` — A page-turning, image-led account of people, tools, colour and cloth.
6. **From the workshop** — Source-linked Instagram reels showing the work in motion.
7. **The textiles** `#textiles` — A horizontal gallery leading to four category pages.
8. **A closer look** `#detail` — An interactive motif study with image zoom.
9. **The artisan archive** `#legacy` — Ram Kishore Chhipa's individually attributed National Award (1987) and Padma Shri (2009), linked to the Rajasthan government profile.
10. **Work with us** `#enquiries` — Three enquiry paths: personal, design/sourcing and international. These invite questions and make no shipping or export promise.
11. **Good to know** `#questions` — Accessible answers about the craft, categories, larger projects and international enquiries.
12. **Contact and footer** `#contact` — WhatsApp, telephone, Instagram, Bagru address and site links.

## Navigation and indexing

The primary navigation highlights Story, Process, Textiles and Legacy, with a persistent Enquire action. The footer links to the additional sections. `app/sitemap.ts` lists public **routes**, so the new on-page sections do not add XML sitemap entries. Before launch, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://rkderawalabagru.com` in the production hosting environment and rebuild to enable canonical URLs, indexing and the public XML sitemap.
